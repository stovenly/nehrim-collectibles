All 22 Almanacs of Conjuration in Nehrim

Nehrim Collectibles Tracker › Almanacs of Conjuration

Almanacs of Conjuration each raise Conjuration by 5 levels, at the cost of 5 learning points. Finding the first one starts the side quest The Books of Conjuration.

All 22 are listed below, counted out of Nehrim.esm rather than copied from a guide. To see only the ones you have not picked up yet, drop your save on the tracker — it works that out from the save and pins what is left on the world map.
