Find the collectibles you are still missing in Nehrim

Nehrim hides five kinds of collectibles. The game counts how many you have picked up, but it never tells you which ones, so tracking down the last few means working through a guide location by location and trying to remember where you have already been.

Load a save here and the page works out which ones you already have. What is left is shown as a list, with the location of each one, and as pins on the world map.

What it tracks

  • 100 Magic Symbols, most of them at the end of a dungeon or cave.
  • 89 Sparks of Fire Caps — 84 plants, 4 loose ingredients and one on the Dwarven Thief’s corpse.
  • 158 Ice Claws — 153 plants, 3 loose ingredients and 2 in the Stoneworld reward barrel.
  • 22 Almanacs of Conjuration, one of them carried by the Keeper of the Last Barrier.
  • 27 Potions of Encumbrance.

The counts are read out of Nehrim.esm, not copied from a wiki, so they include placements the guides have missed.

How it reads a save

Symbols and plants disable themselves when you take them, and loose items are deleted from the world. Either way the save keeps a record of it, and that is what the page reads. Ones held by a corpse or a container are found by checking that inventory instead.

It also compares the game’s own symbol counter against what the save shows, and warns you if the two disagree.

The save is read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is nothing to install.
