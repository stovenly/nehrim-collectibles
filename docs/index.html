Find the collectibles you are still missing in Nehrim

Nehrim hides five kinds of collectibles. The game counts how many you have picked up, but it never tells you which ones, so tracking down the last few means working through a guide location by location and trying to remember where you have already been.

Load a save here and the page works out which ones you already have. What is left is shown as a list, with the location of each one, and as pins on the world map.

What it tracks

  • 100 Magic Symbols
  • 89 Sparks of Fire Caps
  • 158 Ice Claws
  • 22 Almanacs of Conjuration
  • 27 Potions of Encumbrance

It all happens in your browser. Your save never leaves your machine.
