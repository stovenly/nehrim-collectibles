All 100 Magic Symbols in Nehrim

Nehrim Collectibles Tracker › Magic Symbols

Magic Symbols, also spelled magical symbols, are hidden all over Nehrim, most of them waiting at the end of a dungeon or cave. Each one gives experience, and finding every one earns the Magic Nehrim achievement. They glow faintly, so anything catching the light in a dark room is worth a second look.

All 100 are listed below, counted out of Nehrim.esm rather than copied from a guide. To see only the ones you have not picked up yet, drop your save on the tracker — it works that out from the save and pins what is left on the world map.
