All 89 Sparks of Fire Caps in Nehrim

Nehrim Collectibles Tracker › Sparks of Fire Caps

Sparks of Fire Caps are unique plants placed by hand around Nehrim. Eating one raises Luck permanently by 1. A handful exist as loose ingredients rather than as growing plants.

All 89 are listed below, counted out of Nehrim.esm rather than copied from a guide. To see only the ones you have not picked up yet, drop your save on the tracker — it works that out from the save and pins what is left on the world map.
