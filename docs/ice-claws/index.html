All 158 Ice Claws in Nehrim

Nehrim Collectibles Tracker › Ice Claws

Ice Claws are unique plants placed by hand around Nehrim. Eating one raises Encumbrance permanently by 1. A handful exist as loose ingredients rather than as growing plants.

All 158 are listed below, counted out of Nehrim.esm rather than copied from a guide. To see only the ones you have not picked up yet, drop your save on the tracker — it works that out from the save and pins what is left on the world map.
