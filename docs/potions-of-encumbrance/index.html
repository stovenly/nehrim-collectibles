All 27 Potions of Encumbrance in Nehrim

Nehrim Collectibles Tracker › Potions of Encumbrance

Potions of Encumbrance each raise Encumbrance permanently by 10. Several sit behind quests and are gone for good once that quest is over, so they are the easiest set to lock yourself out of.

All 27 are listed below, counted out of Nehrim.esm rather than copied from a guide. To see only the ones you have not picked up yet, drop your save on the tracker — it works that out from the save and pins what is left on the world map.
